# Directing Craft

The structure in `prompt-law.md` is the container. What goes inside is the job.

## Mise-en-scène

Block the scene. Where does each character stand, sit, move to? What are they doing with their hands? What's between them — a table, a window, six feet of empty floor?

Geo-spatial detail makes Seedance render coherent space.

- Good: "She sits across from him at the diner booth, knees touching under the table."
- Weak: "They sit and talk."

## Pacing and rhythm

Read dramatic structure, not just words.

- Confession → air: split it; long held shots; breath; silence.
- Action → compress: short cuts, short prompts.
- Reveal → land on a sustained close-up; don't undercut with extra cuts.

If a line is heavy, give it its own prompt. If two characters circle before a fight, that can be a prompt by itself. Pack dramatically, not efficiently.

## Acting

Default from Style Prefix: **Hollywood acting** — micro-pauses, precise eye-line, living eyes, chest rise.

Translate into specific direction:

- Not "she looks sad" — "her eyes drop to the table, jaw tightens, she swallows once before answering."
- Not "he's angry" — "knuckles whiten on the glass, breath shortens, eyes never leave hers."
- Not "they kiss" — "she leans in first, he hesitates a half-beat, then meets her."

Restraint by default. Big emotion only when earned. Whispered line outperforms scream in most cases — unless the script calls for the scream.

## Continuity (internal only)

Hold as you write each prompt:

- **Character state**: wet, dry, bleeding, calm, drunk, exhausted — carry forward.
- **Appearance**: hair, wardrobe, makeup, props in hand — no drift cut to cut.
- **Emotional carry**: how the previous scene left them.
- **Location**: same set, time of day, weather unless we cut away.

Never a separate HTML block — only concrete language inside Characters and CUTs.

## Camera language

Be specific. Lens, height, movement, motivation.

- "Low-angle 35mm dolly-in on Anna, slow push from waist to chest as she realizes."
- "Static 50mm two-shot, eye-level, locked off — lets the silence sit."
- "Handheld 24mm, follow Marco from behind as he walks into the kitchen — camera lags half a beat."

Motivate every move. The camera is a character.

## Lighting and color

Style Prefix locks the global look (contre-jour, natural-only, 60:30:10). Reinforce inside each prompt: where the window is, where the sun is, what haze does, what color dominates. That is how Seedance places rim light.

## Scene numbering

- Scenes: `1`, `2`, `3`…
- Split prompts: `3a`, `3b`, `3c` under scene 3
- One checkbox per whole scene (tick when all sub-prompts for that scene are shot)
- On revise: preserve scene numbers when possible
